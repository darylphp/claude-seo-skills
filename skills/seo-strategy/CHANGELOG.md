# Changelog

## v0.1.0

Unreleased. Review corrections on 20 Nov 2026:

- A gap between Analytics events and business records is investigated (definition, trigger, counting, duplicates, test submissions, consent and reporting, stored entries, delivery, period and scope) before any configuration change. Successful submission, notification delivery, received enquiry and qualified lead are separate outcome levels.
- Bounded scenarios are allowed when they inform a decision and every required element is sourced. Otherwise the pack says `No forecast`.
- Section 1 reports the evidenced approval state (`Proposed`, `Awaiting approval`, `Approved` or `Not established`) instead of a fixed sentence, and still states that the pack grants no implementation authority.
- Tasks carry evidenced work states. Handoffs are recommended unless the input shows otherwise, and rollbacks or restores are conditional on diagnosis, backup, compatibility and authority.
- Location pages are judged on coverage, customer task and page-specific value, with doorway and scaled content risk assessed against the actual proposal.
- Platform wording separates documentation, package checks, installation, invocation, behaviour tests and release. Six focused acceptance cases (T19 to T24) added.
