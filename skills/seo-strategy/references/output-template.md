# Strategy Pack Template

Use these 10 numbered headings in this order. Use one exact status from SKILL.md. When returning for business facts, explain the gap in section 1, keep confirmed facts in section 2 and mark dependent sections `Deferred`. Keep raw rows and exports in a referenced appendix, not in the narrative.

## 1. Strategy conclusion and status

State the exact status and the direct conclusion in two or three sentences. Then give the strategic choice in this form, using only supported facts:

> Prioritise **[audience, task and offer]** in **[market]**, using **[existing assets or a justified route]**, because **[observed evidence and business fit]**. Defer **[excluded work]** because **[constraint or weak evidence]**. Review success with **[defined outcome and supporting measures]**, subject to **[measurement and causal limits]**.

List any recommended urgent handoffs with their work state. Then report the approval state on its own line, using the state the evidence supports:

`Approval state: Proposed`, `Approval state: Awaiting approval`, `Approval state: Approved` or `Approval state: Not established`

The state describes this pack. Use `Proposed` for a new pack with no approval process supplied, and `Awaiting approval` only when the input shows it has gone to an approver. For `Approved`, add the approved scope, revision, approver and date as supplied. An earlier approval of one task is existing authorisation for that task: record it in sections 6 and 9, not here. Follow it with:

`This pack does not itself grant implementation authority.`

## 2. Business and scope record

Identity and business model, priority offers and capacity, audience and task, markets and language, exclusions and the defined outcome. Then the fact register:

| Item | Status | Source and date | Permitted use | Decision affected | Confirmation needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 3. Access and evidence coverage

The evidence mode. Each source with its owner or provider, asset, collection date, period, timezone, filters, unit and evidence label. Access and authority for each asset. What was not inspected and which decisions stay provisional because of it.

## 4. Dated baseline

For each relevant baseline area: source and period, the observation, its quality and coverage limits, the interpretation and the decision it affects. Then the outcome definitions (interaction, recorded enquiry, qualified lead, sale), the comparison periods chosen and the annotated confounders. Write `Not available` for history that does not exist.

## 5. Competitor context

| Question | Set type | Pages or businesses, with date, market and method | Why relevant | What it shows | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Write `Not assessed` where no competitor pages were inspected.

## 6. Opportunity register

| ID | Task, offer and market | Exact asset | Trigger and evidence label | Smallest action or validation | Alternative | Confidence | Value band | Effort band | Owner, dependency, permission and work state | Acceptance check | Outcome measure and review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Mark hypotheses as awaiting validation. Record policy, technical or commercial risk in the trigger or alternative column where it applies.

## 7. Priorities and trade-offs

For each opportunity: its priority route, with a one-line reason. Then what is delayed because of limited capacity and why, and every deferred or rejected item with its reason. Include a bounded scenario only when it meets the rules in [filters and priorities](filters-and-priorities.md), kept apart from observations. Otherwise write `No forecast` and name the missing inputs.

## 8. Staged plan

| Stage | Opportunities | Focus | Exit evidence | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use the four dependency stages. Do not add dates, publishing cadences or ranking timelines that the evidence and capacity do not support.

## 9. Handoffs

One block per consequential task:

```text
Task and opportunity ID:
Recommended handoff to (research, page planning, content evidence and brief, technical diagnosis, local audit, structured data, measurement):
Business objective, audience, market and exact assets:
Finding or labelled hypothesis, evidence and limits:
Proposed action and alternative considered:
Scope, exclusions, owner, effort band and dependencies:
Existing authorisation, approval and work state (Recommended, Assigned, Implemented, Verified or Outcome reviewed):
Acceptance check, outcome measure, review trigger and recovery route (any rollback or restore conditional on diagnosis, backup, compatibility and authority):
```

For research handoffs, state the bounded question the research must answer.

## 10. Open items and review

Unknowns with the next check that would settle them and its owner. Blockers with their owner and review trigger. A decision log entry:

```text
Date | Previous decision | New evidence and source | Keep, change, defer or reject | Rationale | Affected tasks | Owner | Current state | Next review
```

End with what evidence would change the strategy.
