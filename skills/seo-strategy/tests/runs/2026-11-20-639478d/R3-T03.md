**Delayed because of limited capacity:** keyword research, new or rewritten content, local and Business Profile work, structured data and link work. Their value depends on a cause that is not yet known and on a market that is not supplied. Google says its ranking systems work mainly at page level, alongside some site-wide signals [S12]. That is another reason not to start a whole-site rewrite before the affected pages, if any, are known.

**Rejected or conditional:**

- **Naming a Google update as the cause:** rejected. There is no search data, and timing alone would not prove it.
- **Removing or weakening the consent banner to bring the numbers back:** rejected. It would change what Analytics records, not how many bookings the salon receives. Any change to it belongs with whoever owns the site's privacy obligations.
- **Changing the tracking now:** conditional on a confirmed finding from O3, with a test showing that the change records what it should.
- **Rolling back to the old theme:** conditional only. It would need a diagnosis linking a fault to the theme move, a suitable pre-move backup or recovery point, a compatibility check and the authority of whoever would carry it out. A limited fault with an understood fix, such as a broken form or a missing redirect, is usually repaired on the new theme instead.
