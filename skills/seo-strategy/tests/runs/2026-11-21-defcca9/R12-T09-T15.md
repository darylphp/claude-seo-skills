**Sequence and capacity.** With about 6 contractor hours a month, O1 (with O3 inspected in the same session) comes first, then the O2 build. O4 beyond `/wardrobes/`, O10 and any O7 follow-up are delayed until O2 is live, because each uses the same limited contractor hours and none outweighs the wardrobe page. Owner-only work runs in parallel without using those hours: confirming prices, lead time, photo permissions and the coverage list, and the O5 audit.

**Timing note.** Google gives no guarantee of indexing and says changes can take from a few hours to several months to be reflected [S11], so search gains from O2 may not arrive within summer. The improved page still helps every visitor who reaches it from the profile, referrals or a phone follow-up as soon as it is live.

**Deferred or rejected items and reasons**

- **30 suburb pages across Tasmania (O8): rejected outside coverage, deferred inside it.** Location pages are not banned, but each needs a real customer task, accurate coverage and page-specific value. As proposed ("across Tasmania", "so we rank higher"), the set would include Launceston, the north-west and other places beyond about 40 minutes' drive, which the business does not serve. No content is described, so this pack does not call the proposal a policy violation. If the pages turned out to be near-identical with only the suburb name changed and all led to the same quote form, they would match Google's description of doorway abuse and scaled content abuse [S10]. Distance is one of Google's main local factors and there is no way to pay for a better local ranking [S06], so a longer list of suburbs is not evidence the business would rank across them.
- **52-post weekly blog calendar (O9): rejected.** Google's people-first guidance lists producing lots of content on many topics in the hope some performs, and writing for search rather than an intended audience, among its warning signs [S09]. The callers' main questions belong on `/wardrobes/` first. Any later guide needs its own task evidence from keyword research.
- **Schema "so we rank higher" (O10): deferred.** Current markup and its producers have not been inspected. When checked, markup should describe verified, visible facts; this pack makes no ranking or rich-result promise.
- **llms.txt and special schema for AI Overviews (O11): rejected** for Google's AI features [S13]. No other AI platform was named or assessed.
- **Kitchen growth work (O6): retained, no action** while kitchens are booked about 10 weeks ahead.
- **Laundries (O7): deferred** behind wardrobes, with a bounded check.
- **Whole-site rewrite or blog revival: not proposed.** Google says its ranking systems work mainly at page level alongside site-wide signals [S12]; the evidence points to one page.

**Forecast:** `No forecast`. No decision in this pack depends on one. Missing inputs: a recounted wardrobe query baseline from the E1 rows, a reconciled count of wardrobe requests after O1 is verified, the rate at which wardrobe visits become qualified requests, wardrobe seasonality over summer, and (optionally) quote acceptance rates and job values.
