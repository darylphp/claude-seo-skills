**Deferred or rejected items**

- **O6, 30 suburb pages across Tasmania (rejected as proposed):** As described, the set would include places the business does not serve (Launceston and the north-west are excluded, and "across Tasmania" reaches far beyond about 40 minutes' drive from Moonah), its stated purpose is ranking, and no page-specific content, local job evidence or distinct customer task has been identified. Google's spam policies describe doorway abuse as pages made to rank for similar queries that lead users to less useful intermediate pages, and scaled content abuse as many pages made mainly to manipulate rankings rather than help users [S10]. No page content exists to assess, so this pack does not call the proposal a violation, but the described set carries that risk and has no supported benefit. Pages for unserved places would also invite more out-of-scope enquiries like the two Launceston calls in E6. Google names relevance, distance and prominence as the main local ranking factors and says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking [S06], so a longer list of places is not evidence the business would rank across them.
- **O7, more blog posts (deferred):** Google's people-first guidance lists producing lots of content on many topics in the hope some performs, and writing to a word count, as warning signs [S09]. The questions callers ask (price, lead time, door type) are service-page questions and go on `/wardrobes/` first.
- **O8, schema (deferred):** In this pack, structured data describes visible verified facts. No source in the register supports it as a way to rank higher, and Google says no special markup is needed for its generative AI features [S13]. It returns as a check after O1 is live.
- **O5, kitchen growth (deferred):** More kitchen demand would meet a full schedule.
