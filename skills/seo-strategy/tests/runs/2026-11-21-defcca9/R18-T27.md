**Main sensitivities and what would invalidate it**

1. **The Search Console conflict (largest).** On E1's figures, `/wardrobes/` received about 3 to 4 clicks per six months. 2. **Transfer of U.** One page, one release and a different product; the laundries change could have had other causes.

5. **Timing.** Quotes, wins and installs lag enquiries, so jobs from May enquiries may fall after the period.

These are modelled differences between cases, not measured impact, and none is a forecast of what will happen.

**What the scenario means for the decision (recommendation).** Do it now. The modelled gain is small and could be zero, and the measurement conflict makes even that range low confidence.
