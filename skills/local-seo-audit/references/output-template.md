# Audit Output Template

Use these 10 numbered headings in this order. Use one exact status from SKILL.md. For a blocked or escalated audit, explain the gate in section 1, keep confirmed facts, and mark sections that depend on the missing input as Deferred.

## 1. Audit status and scope

Record the exact status, the location and profile identifier, the audit date and evidence cut-off, the evidence basis (public only, account exports, business-confirmed facts), the areas covered and not covered, and the main limitation. Then, on its own line: `Implementation approval: Awaiting approval`. Replace it only when a valid approval for the exact actions was supplied, naming the approver, role, date and the action IDs it covers.

## 2. Business record

Record the trading name, business model, objective and customer action, market, approved facts with who confirmed them and when, and access and permissions as known. List missing facts.

## 3. Evidence inspected

| Source | What it showed | Date | Label | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

List the checks that were needed but are Not verified, and why.

## 4. Eligibility, representation and access

Record the eligibility position for this model, the address or service-area treatment, and ownership, consent and verification state as evidenced. Note any hold on profile changes.

## 5. Profile accuracy

| Field | Observed | Approved fact | Finding | Evidence and confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include phone and contact-path checks.

## 6. Reviews and reputation

Record the request process, reply authority, sample observations and policy risks.

## 7. Website, local pages, citations and structured data

Record the profile destination, findings on location or service-area pages, material citation errors, and structured data producers and eligibility notes.

## 8. Measurement and visibility baseline

Record the available metrics and their definitions, tracking state, any visibility sampling method, and what the data can and cannot show.

## 9. Prioritised findings and actions

| ID | Priority | Area | Finding | Evidence and confidence | Smallest action | Owner and approval needed | Acceptance check | Review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Order by priority, then customer impact. List no-action decisions separately with the reason.

## 10. Open questions and handoffs

List each unknown with the evidence that would resolve it and who can supply it, the handoffs to other workflows, and the state of each action: proposed, approved, submitted, publicly verified or outcome-reviewed. Proposed is the default.
