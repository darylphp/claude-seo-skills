# Input Requirements

Accept relevant extracts or equivalent records. Keep their IDs, dates and revisions where supplied. Use one record per location.

| Input | Minimum usable detail |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Real-world trading name, the location being audited, its public profile or Maps URL and the website URL. Add the account profile ID or store code when available. |
| Business model | Storefront, service-area or hybrid, with where customers are actually served and whether the base receives customers during stated hours. |
| Objective | The customer action that matters (call, booking, visit or enquiry), the priority offer, the market and any delivery limits. |
| Approved facts | Name, address or private base, service areas, hours, phone, website, categories, services and attributes the business confirms, with who confirmed them and when. |
| Access and permission | Who owns the profile, any manager or agency role, consent to manage, and approval to reply to reviews. Note what the requester is allowed to change. |
| Evidence | What was inspected, when and how: public profile screenshots or text, account exports, website pages or a crawl, Search Console, analytics, call or booking records, review request materials and review samples, and rank or grid samples with their method. |
| Known changes | Edits, moves, rebrands, site releases, tracking changes, campaigns, agency changes or seasonality in the period. |

Optional evidence may be missing. Record the gap and its effect on the conclusion. A business name alone, a keyword and a city, a competitor's profile, a rank screenshot with no method or a completeness score is not enough to audit.

Never ask for or keep passwords, verification codes, unredacted identity documents or customer personal data. If the user supplies them, leave them out of the report and recommend storing them in an approved restricted location.

## Return paths

- **Blocked: Confirm the business and location** when the business, location or profile cannot be identified with confidence, when two candidate profiles match and nothing confirms which belongs to the business, when several locations are in scope and none is selected, or when the task needs account access or changes and the requester's relationship to the business is unknown. Name the missing fact and who can supply it.
- **Blocked: Supply audit evidence** when the business and location are clear but nothing can be inspected: no profile evidence, no website, no exports, and no permission or ability to open public pages. Name the smallest evidence set that would let the audit start.
- If both apply, report the identity block first and list the evidence needed for the next pass.
- **Escalate first: Profile incident** takes precedence over a ready report. See [action rules](action-rules.md), section 3.
- Unresolved eligibility does not block the audit. Put profile creation and identity-sensitive profile changes on hold for eligibility, and continue with the website, reputation and measurement checks that still help.
- For a batch, keep each location's record, findings and approval separate. Do not present a group total in place of location-level findings.
