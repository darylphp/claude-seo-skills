# Action Rules

IDs in brackets refer to the [source register](source-register.md). Text marked **Google guidance** paraphrases that source. Everything else is operating method.

## 1. Priorities

These are operating priorities, not Google severity labels. Adjust for business impact and dependencies.

| Priority | Examples | What the report says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Unauthorised access, a suspended profile, a false closure, a phone, address or contact route that misdirects customers or is outside the business's control, a broken main enquiry route | Name an owner now, preserve evidence, and correct or escalate within existing permission |
| High | An eligibility conflict, a misleading name or category, a review-policy breach, materially wrong services, hours, coverage or location, an exposed private address | Resolve before relying on the affected profile state or approving related work |
| Planned | A useful page or profile improvement, clearer customer information, a measurement gap, a measurable conversion problem | Brief the smallest change with evidence and an acceptance check |
| Optional | A new feature or convenience with no demonstrated problem | Pilot only if the benefit outweighs the cost and the maintenance |

## 2. Smallest justified action

Choose one: correct, improve, test, monitor, defer or no action. Prefer correcting a clear, evidenced error over a growth experiment. Group related profile edits into one controlled change. Do not recommend repeated category changes, overwriting pending edits or changing identity fields as a ranking test. Record no action when the evidence does not justify a change.

For each action, record:

- the exact current value and proposed value, or the page and the change
- the evidence, confidence and any competing explanation
- the owner if known, the permission already held and the approval still needed
- dependencies and risks, including verification exposure [G06, G07]
- an acceptance check a person can observe, such as the public profile showing the approved hours on a named date
- the metric or review trigger for judging the outcome later

**Google guidance [G19]:** profile edits can be accepted, pending or rejected, and review can take longer than usual. An unchanged public value soon after submission is not proof of rejection. **Google guidance [G20]:** some suggested edits have a short response window after notification, so recommend a named alert owner and a backup.

## 3. Incident route

Use the status **Escalate first: Profile incident** when the evidence shows any of the cases below. Hold growth recommendations until the incident is handled, and keep confirmed facts in the report.

| Incident | Supported boundary | What to report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suspended or disabled profile | Fix evidenced issues, then use Google's appeal route with genuine evidence. Do not create another profile for the same business [G21]. | Exact status and any stated reason, evidence to gather, who can submit, and what not to do |
| Suspected duplicate | Separate the same business from distinct eligible entities. Merges need review [G22]. | Profile URLs or IDs, the identity comparison and the recommended route |
| Relocation | Update the existing profile rather than create a new one [G22]. | Address, website, phone, citation and verification steps to coordinate |
| Ownership conflict or unauthorised access | Use the ownership request or support route [G05]. | Who holds rights, what to secure first and what remains unknown |
| Harmful false closure or contact detail | Correct it through the authorised account, then check the public result [G19]. | Exact field, approved value, owner and the public check |

Never recommend recreating a profile to avoid enforcement, fabricating documents or promising reinstatement.

## 4. Handoffs

Name the next workflow when the work belongs elsewhere: technical SEO diagnosis, keyword or page-opportunity research, a content brief, structured data implementation or measurement setup. The audit states what the handoff needs. It does not do that work.
