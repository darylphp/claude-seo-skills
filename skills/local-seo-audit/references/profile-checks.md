# Profile Checks

IDs in brackets refer to the [source register](source-register.md). Text marked **Google guidance** paraphrases that source. Everything else is operating method.

## 1. Eligibility and representation

**Google guidance [G01, G02]:** a business needs in-person contact with customers during its stated hours, with listed exceptions. Online-only businesses and lead-generation businesses are ineligible. Only owners or authorised representatives may manage a profile. The profile name should match the real-world name, without taglines, keywords, location or hours text. A mailing-only virtual office is ineligible. A co-working office needs clear signage, customers received there and the business's own staff during stated hours. A business that shows its address should keep permanent signage there.

Check the actual operating model before judging the profile:

| Model | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Storefront | The public address matches customer-facing premises with signage, staff and access during stated hours. |
| Service-area | The business travels to customers and does not receive them at its base, so the address should be hidden [G01, G03]. Never recommend publishing a private home address. |
| Hybrid | The business receives customers at the address during stated hours and also travels or delivers. Both arrangements are real and evidenced. |
| Practitioner, department, chain or brand | Apply the specific representation rule in G01 before recommending an edit or a new profile. |

**Google guidance [G03, G01]:** service areas are listed as cities, postcodes or other areas, not a radius, up to 20. The overall area should usually stay within about two hours' drive of the base, though some businesses can justify a larger area.

Compare the listed areas with where the business genuinely accepts work. Do not fill every slot. A service area is not a branch, and adding a town does not guarantee visibility there.

An unresolved eligibility question puts profile creation and identity-sensitive changes on hold. It does not stop the website, reputation or measurement checks.

## 2. Access, consent and verification

**Google guidance [G04]:** a third party needs the owner's express consent to claim or manage a profile, and explicit approval to reply to reviews. Verbal consent is not enough for review replies. The business should own the profile and invite the agency as a manager. Passwords should not be shared, and top placement must not be promised.

Check:

- Search for an existing profile before suggesting a new one. Compare identity and location, not only similar names.
- Who owns and manages the profile, and whether the requester's role covers the proposed actions.
- If someone else controls a verified profile, route to Google's ownership request process [G05] without promising the outcome.
- Verification state comes from the account or the business, not the public listing. An attempted verification is not a confirmed one.
- Name, category and address changes can affect verification [G06, G07]. Flag that exposure before recommending them.

## 3. Core fields

For each field, record the observed value, the approved value, the gap and the evidence label.

| Field | Check | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Matches the name on signage, stationery and the website [G01]. | Keywords, places, hours or taglines added to the name. |
| Primary category | The most specific category for what the business is [G01, G07]. | Copying a competitor's category or using categories as keywords. |
| Other categories | Genuine additional business types only [G01]. | A category for every service, product or amenity. |
| Address or pin | Real location and the correct public or hidden treatment for the model [G01]. | A PO box, virtual office or exposed private address. |
| Hours | Customer-facing hours, time zone, public holidays and special hours as the business confirms them. | Invented 24-hour or emergency availability. |
| Phone | Reaches this location, is under the business's direct control, local where possible and not a premium number [G01]. Test only with authorisation. | Assuming a tracking number is compliant or routes correctly. |
| Website link | Goes to the page that best represents this location and its customer task. See the website checks. | Swapping a useful homepage link for a thin town page. |
| Description | Useful facts about the business, no links or HTML, not focused on promotions, prices or sales, within the field limit [G01, G06]. | Treating a keyword list as optimisation. |
| Services | Only services offered now, in the intended fields [G08]. | Services the approved facts do not confirm. |
| Attributes | Each value confirmed by the business. | Unknown recorded as yes. |
| Photos and video | Truthful, authorised and representative of the real business. | Generated or stock images presented as the premises, staff or completed work. |
| Posts and action links | Current, useful and working. Expired offers removed. Unexpected third-party action links reviewed [G09]. | A posting quota presented as a ranking requirement. |

A completeness indicator is a maintenance prompt, not proof of a well-run profile. Do not recommend edits just to create activity.

## 4. Phone numbers and call tracking

A tracking number still has to meet the phone rules in G01. A vendor feature or a competitor's setup does not show compliance. Before recommending or keeping a tracking number, check:

- who controls the number and the provider account, and how the business would recover it
- where it routes, including after hours, voicemail and failure
- every place the number appears: profile, website, `tel:` links, ads, citations and structured data
- how calls are counted, and that call-button clicks, provider-recorded calls, answered calls and qualified enquiries stay separate

**Google guidance [G10]:** the GBP Calls metric counts clicks on the call button, not answered calls. Keeping or restoring the business's own verified number is a valid outcome when control or routing cannot be shown.
