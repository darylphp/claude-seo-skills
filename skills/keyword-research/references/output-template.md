# Research Pack Template

Use these 9 numbered headings in this order. Use one exact readiness status from SKILL.md. When returning for scope clarification, explain the gap in section 1, keep confirmed facts and mark dependent sections Deferred. Keep raw rows in a referenced table or appendix, not in the narrative.

## 1. Research conclusion and readiness

State the exact status, the direct conclusion in two or three sentences, the readiness of each family where it differs, and the main limit. Then, on its own line: `Page and URL decisions: Deferred to planning`.

## 2. Scope, mode and sources

Record the research question, mode, business and offers, audience, market, language, device assumptions, inclusions and exclusions, and the source inventory with status, period, settings and limits.

## 3. Dataset coverage

Record row counts and processing coverage only where actually measured, the deduplication applied and any rows not processed. Write `Not available` for unknown coverage.

## 4. Family map

| Family ID | Name | Representative queries and row IDs | Reader task and next action | Stage and intent | Constraints | Evidence status and confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep brand or navigational families separate. Note mixed intent and secondary tags.

## 5. Result evidence and open boundaries

For each observation: query, date, market and location setting, device, method, what was observed and which family it supports or contradicts. List families without observations and give their validation plan. List unresolved boundaries.

## 6. Entities and decision criteria

| Family ID | Entity, attribute or criterion | Class | Why it matters | Evidence required or source | Uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 7. Specific-task and gap candidates

List specific-task, long-tail and competitor-gap candidates with source, evidence status, business fit, demand evidence and missing proof. State "Not assessed" where no competitor pages were inspected.

## 8. Exclusions, missing evidence and corrections

List excluded items with reasons, missing evidence with who can supply it, and corrections needed in supplied data.

## 9. Planning handoff

For each family ready or provisionally ready:

```text
Family ID, name and representative queries:
Reader task, outcome, intent and next action:
Meaningful variants, urgency and constraints:
Observed result types and supporting URLs:
Candidate role(s), for the planning decision only:
Source-labelled metrics and limits:
Decision criteria and entities:
Observed existing URL signals:
Evidence and useful contribution expected:
Ambiguity, exclusions and restrictions:
Confidence and next check:
```

End with the restrictions that apply to all families and where the raw table, source records and exclusion log are kept.
