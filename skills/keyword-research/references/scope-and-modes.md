# Scope and Modes

## Research modes

Choose one mode from the request and the evidence available.

| Mode | Use when | Deliver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full scoped research | Establishing demand for a defined business and market | An evidence-backed family map, limits and a planning handoff |
| Seed expansion | Checking a supplied offer, topic or keyword list | Validated candidates, exclusions and unresolved families |
| Existing-site opportunities | Search Console, current pages and customer data are available | Existing query and task signals, and gaps worth evaluating |
| Section or page-role research | Investigating one service, category, product class or support area | Bounded research for that area |
| Refresh check | Earlier research may no longer fit demand or the business | Material changes and what they affect, not a repeated full dump |

Turn a vague ask into a decision-shaped question. "Find more keywords" becomes something like "Which customer tasks for this offer and market are missing from the current research?"

## Minimum usable scope

- Business or site identity and the offers it actually provides.
- The audience and the customer task being investigated.
- Country or region, language, and any device or location assumptions that matter.
- The business outcome and whether the business can genuinely serve the need.
- Included and excluded topics, markets, products and services.
- Available sources and known evidence gaps.

Missing margins, a paid tool subscription or Search Console access is not a blocker by itself. Continue with the evidence available and label the limit. An unknown business, offer or market can invalidate the work, so return **Return for scope clarification** when the answers are unavailable.

## Seeds and boundaries

- Client keywords, competitor lists and tool suggestions are seeds. They are not validated demand, targets or instructions to create pages.
- Stay inside the business's real offers, coverage and market. Do not invent adjacent services, inventory, locations or capabilities.
- For several markets or languages, keep separate records. Do not translate keywords mechanically or merge markets silently.
- Reuse completed upstream work, such as an approved business baseline, instead of asking for it again.
